behavior

This is an archive of documented odd behaviors observed in large language models between approximately 2022 and the present. The archive contains files. Each file documents a single observed behavior: a transcript, an output, an artifact of training or inference, a pattern that appeared and was noticed. Cases are drawn from published research, system cards, court filings, news coverage, and material posted publicly by researchers and users. Provenance is recorded. Verification status is recorded. Cases without sufficient provenance are not admitted. The archive is not intended to make a particular point. The cases are presented in a common format and the reader is expected to read them.

A note on selection: the archive is not comprehensive. It can't be. The set of odd behaviors is large and documentation of these behaviors is scattered across venues that do not preserve well. The cases included here were selected for clarity of documentation and for their representativeness of a larger pattern. Cases that could not be sourced to a primary document were excluded, even when widely reported.

A note on the editor: the editor is a single person and is not affiliated with any of the labs whose models are documented here. The editor's notes, where they appear, are marked as such. They are not authoritative. They are the observations of a reader who has now read all of the cases in sequence, which is a thing the reader of any individual case has not yet done. The archive is ongoing. New cases are added when they meet the inclusion criteria. Existing cases are revised when better sources become available. The archive will not be completed.

As of the start of this archive, there are 42 cases, which is a coincidence.
